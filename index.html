The process

How the
boot camp works.

01

Apply & get placed

Fill out a short form — no portfolio required. We place you in the right cohort based on your level and track of interest.

02

Learn by building

Every week you work on real projects — not just watching videos. Lectures are short; making things is long. That's by design.

03

Get feedback, fast

Instructors review your work and give specific, honest critiques. You iterate. That's how designers actually grow.

04

Graduate with a portfolio

On the final weekend you present your work in a showcase. You leave with 3+ case studies and the confidence to use them.
